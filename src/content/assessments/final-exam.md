## The brief

> Everything the midterm covered still applies. On top of it: equilibrium
> play, multi-way pots, tournament equity, and bankroll math — the parts of
> the course that only make sense once the earlier weeks are load-bearing
> under them.

The final exam is comprehensive but weighted towards weeks 7 through 12:
mixed strategies and bluffing frequency, GTO against exploitative play,
why the two-player equilibrium results stop being clean once a third
player sits down, ICM tournament equity, and the Kelly criterion. At least
one question on the paper asks you to connect a result from the first half
of the semester to one from the second — for instance, using an
equity-and-EV calculation from week 3 or 4 inside a later equilibrium or
ICM argument — because the course's own claim is that these pieces fit
together, and the exam should ask you to show that, not just recite each
piece separately.

## What you submit

Written answers in the exam booklet, in the room, in the scheduled slot,
under the same conditions as the midterm.
