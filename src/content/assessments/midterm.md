## The brief

> Under exam conditions, do by hand what the weekly assignments let you do
> with time to think.

The midterm sits in week 7 and covers weeks 1 through 6: combinatorics and
combo counting, exact hypergeometric equity, pot odds and EV, position and
information value, and range reading as Bayesian updating. Questions look
like the weekly assignments — one computed quantity, working shown — but
under time pressure and without notes beyond a supplied formula sheet, to
test that the method is understood rather than looked up.

## What you submit

Written answers in the exam booklet, in the room, in the scheduled slot.
No submission format to choose — showing up with a pen is the format.
