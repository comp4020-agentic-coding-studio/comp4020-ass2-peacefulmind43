Renata Sokal designed this course around a single, testable question: how
much of what strong players call instinct is actually a theorem nobody wrote
down. Before Slop University, she worked on equilibrium computation for
small imperfect-information games, which is a longer way of saying she has
spent a lot of time watching solvers agree with people who have never seen a
solver.

Office hours are posted on the course calendar. Email is for anything that
can't wait until the next Problem Session.
