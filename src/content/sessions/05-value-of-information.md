## Before the session

Read the lecture page. Bring the $10 blind / $40 informed / $30
value-of-information numbers — the session checks them by hand before
moving on.

## In the session

The group recomputes the lecture's toy example from the raw payoff table,
confirming +$10 blind and $40 informed before anyone is told the answer.
Then, working in pairs, everyone builds a second toy payoff table with
different numbers and different state probabilities, computes its value of
information, and swaps with another pair to check the arithmetic.

Closing discussion: why is the value of information always ≥ 0? Groups
argue it from the payoff table's structure directly, not from having seen
the lecture's result stated as a fact.

## Afterwards

Nothing is marked this week. Going into week 6: comfort computing value of
information for an unseen payoff table, since week 6 immediately extends
this into partial (not perfect) signals.
