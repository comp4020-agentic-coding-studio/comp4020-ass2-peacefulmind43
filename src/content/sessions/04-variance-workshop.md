## Before the session

Read the lecture page. Bring the flagship hand's 9-out, 19.6% equity figure
— the session builds directly on it rather than re-deriving it.

## In the session

The group recomputes EV(call) for the lecture's bigger-pot version of the
flagship hand (pot $300, call $50), checking it against the lecture's stated
+$18.48 by hand. Then everyone computes the variance and standard deviation
of that same call from the two-outcome formula, and sits with the fact that
an $18.48 edge sits inside a $139 swing — most individual attempts at this
exact call still lose the $50.

Second half: a show of hands on "if you ran this exact call 10 times and
lost 9, would you believe the call was wrong?" before working out the
standard error at N = 10 and seeing why that outcome is unremarkable.

## Afterwards

Nothing is marked this week. Going into week 5, what should be settled:
computing EV and variance from a two-outcome payoff directly, not from
memory of one worked example.
