## Before the session

Read the lecture page. Bring the $100 pot / $50 bet numbers — 25% optimal
bluff frequency, 66.7% MDF — the session re-derives both from the raw EV
equations rather than starting from the ratios.

## In the session

The group sets EV(call) = EV(fold) and EV(bet-as-bluff) = EV(check) from a
blank page, solving both down to x\* = bet/(pot+bet) and c\* =
pot/(pot+bet) before checking against the lecture's numbers.

Then everyone repeats the whole derivation at a new bet size (a pot-sized
bet rather than a half-pot bet), predicting first whether the bluff
frequency should go up or down before doing the algebra, then checking the
prediction against the number.

## Afterwards

Nothing is marked this week. Going into week 8: comfort setting up an
indifference equation for an unseen bet size, since week 8 builds the full
GTO definition directly on top of this same idea.
