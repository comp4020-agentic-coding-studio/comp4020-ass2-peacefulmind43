## Before the session

Watch the deck once through. Don't try to memorize the flagship hand's
numbers — the session rebuilds them from the board up.

## In the session

The group re-derives the flagship hand's outs count, exact equity, pot-odds
threshold, and EV(call) from scratch, checking each step against the deck's
stated answer before moving to the next. Then the same four-step process
(outs → equity → threshold → EV) gets pointed at a second hand nobody has
seen before, with a different draw type and different bet sizing, to check
the method generalizes rather than having been memorized for one board.

The card-removal miscount from the deck gets its own few minutes: everyone
tries the naive 13-minus-board-spades count first, on purpose, before being
shown why it overshoots.

## Afterwards

Nothing is marked this week. What should be settled going into week 4: the
four-step decision process, and comfort with the exact hypergeometric
formula behind step two.
