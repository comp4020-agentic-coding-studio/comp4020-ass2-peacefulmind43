This week's lecture is the [slide deck](/decks/week-03/) rather than this
page — it's the point in the syllabus where the material is genuinely a
sequence of worked steps building on each other, and slides carry that
better than prose. This page is the map of what's in it and what to do
around it.

## What the deck resolves

Week 1 named "use the rule of 4 and 2 to estimate your equity on a draw" as
a claim to be cashed in later. The deck derives the exact hypergeometric
equity the rule is approximating, measures the rule's actual error at
several out counts, and shows the error grows with the size of the draw
rather than staying fixed.

## What's in the deck

Nine steps, in order: the rule restated, its error quantified, outs counted
across draw types (gutshot, open-ended, flush, combo), a common
card-removal miscount and what it costs, the exact hypergeometric formula,
the pot-odds threshold formula, implied and reverse-implied odds, one real
hand walked end to end, and a closing dollar-EV comparison against what the
week 1 folk rule would have priced the same hand at.
