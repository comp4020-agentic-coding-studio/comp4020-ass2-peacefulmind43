Twelve dated lectures, one for each teaching week. Each opens by naming the
earlier week it revises or extends, so the sequence is cumulative rather than
modular — a result from week 3 is still load-bearing in week 10. Week 3
carries the slide deck, where the exact-outs calculation is worked one step
per slide.
