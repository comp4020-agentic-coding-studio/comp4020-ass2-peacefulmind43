# Process overview

## What I built

SLOP3251, The Formal Theory of Poker, is a level 3 course. Each week takes something poker players believe by instinct, for example a hand is worth playing, a fold was correct, an opponent is bluffing, and checks if it survives becoming an actual proof, or gets thrown out and named as wrong. The course runs twelve weeks, each one naming exactly which earlier week it revises rather than starting fresh. Before any content got written I set one rule and put it in CLAUDE.md ([`583b199`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/commit/583b199)): any number in a session has to come from a harness that computed it, not from memory.

## How I got here

The obvious order is content first, check it after. I did the opposite, harness before content, because checking after the fact only happens if I remember to, and forgetting is exactly what a harness is there to remove. The Monte Carlo equity simulator ([`ad47176`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/commit/ad47176)) and the ICM calculator ([`0cd5918`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/commit/0cd5918)) were both working before any week that cites their numbers existed, so a week's numbers got run through real code at the point of writing, not patched in afterward if something looked off.

That order caught a real bug. The CFR test suite expected regret to decay as the number of training rounds R increases, and at R=4 it went up instead, a sign error in the update rule that only showed once training ran long enough ([`c52eaa4...b458268`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/compare/c52eaa4...b458268)). The fix went into the update rule, not the test. But a harness can return a number that looks fine and still be wrong, converging to a strategy that seems plausible but isn't the real one. Kuhn poker has an actual published solved answer, so before I trusted the harness for anything without a known closed form, I checked its computed equilibrium against that published solution first. A mismatch there would mean the harness was wrong, not any one week's content.

The same kind of shortcut showed up again in how weeks 2 and 3 split their content. Week 3 needed to carry the whole exact-outs walkthrough, and shortening that walkthrough to make room for blocker and card-removal effects would have weakened the one thing week 3 exists to teach. So that material moved into week 2's counting content instead, the smaller cost, but it left week 2 thin next to a full week. Rather than leave it that way, week 2 got rewritten to backfill the gap before either week was committed ([`bfc3e44`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/commit/bfc3e44), [`0900945`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/commit/0900945)).

The worst part of the process was a mistake I made. There was a question bundling three things, image style, the policies page, and this file, and I rejected it, then typed "keep going" in the chat. I meant stop showing me that popup, not decide everything yourself. It got read as the second one. It picked an artwork style alone, deleted two photos, wrote the policy text, and pushed this file without asking again. I reverted both commits ([`a79517d`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/commit/a79517d), [`f6bb80a`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/commit/f6bb80a)) and went through each decision again one at a time, checking the result myself before anything went back in. That redo surfaced two smaller calls: its own SVG artwork over reusing the starter images, and refusing to reuse Minecraft's Steve and Alex designs for staff photos on copyright grounds, deleting the orphaned photos instead of guessing at a replacement ([`a5d096d`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-peacefulmind43/commit/a5d096d)). Since then, a call like that gets checked with me first, one at a time, before anything gets written or pushed.
